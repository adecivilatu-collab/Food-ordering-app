# **PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)**

## **1\. Product Overview**

### **Product Type**

Multi-restaurant online food ordering and delivery marketplace.

### **Product Concept**

The platform will allow customers to discover restaurants in their delivery area, browse menus, order meals, make payments, track deliveries, and review their experiences.

Restaurants will have their own presence on the platform and will be able to manage their menus, prices, operating hours, promotions, and incoming orders.

The platform will also coordinate the delivery experience between restaurants, customers, and delivery riders.

### **Primary Market**

The initial focus will be the Nigerian market, with the ability to expand into additional cities and markets over time.

### **Product Vision**

To create a convenient, trusted and easy-to-use food marketplace that connects customers with restaurants and reliable delivery services.

# **2\. Product Goals**

The platform should:

* Make ordering food simple and convenient.  
* Give customers access to multiple restaurants in one place.  
* Help restaurants reach more customers and increase orders.  
* Provide reliable delivery experiences.  
* Support both digital payments and cash on delivery.  
* Make order progress easy to understand.  
* Encourage repeat purchases and customer loyalty.  
* Give restaurants useful tools to manage their businesses.  
* Build trust through ratings, reviews and transparent pricing.  
* Create a marketplace that can expand across Nigerian cities.
